// About
Who I Am

I'm a graduate researcher at the University of Arkansas Cognitive Robotics Lab and the founder of Kinetic Intelligence Labs. My work sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, embedded control systems, and robotic actuator design.

Before grad school, I spent six years as an Army aviation electrician maintaining helicopter avionics on UH-60 and UH-72 airframes, which gave me a deep respect for safety-critical systems and rigorous test procedures.

My goal is to bridge the gap between academic robotics research and commercially viable robotic hardware — bringing RL-trained motor control from simulation to production-grade embedded systems.
